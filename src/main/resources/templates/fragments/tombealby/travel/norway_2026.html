Norway 2026
Exploring the Fjords

In July 2026 we took a short trip to Norway to explore the Norwegian fjords. This page contains highlights of that trip.

We explored southern Norway, visiting the towns of Stavanger, Olden, Hellesylt and Haugesund.

Our first day in Norway was spent in Stavanger. We went on a boat ride around Lysefjord. It was a bright sunny day and the views were very good.

We visited the Briksdal glacier from Olden. To get to the glacier we had a short coach ride before getting on troll cars for the final part of the journey. This photograh was taken from the coach.

In Haugesund we took a two hour coach journey to view the surrouding countryside, before embarking on a boat to take us back to Haugesund.
